SF'S GREATEST HITS

some of our favs for those wondering

ACTIVITIES

  • Soak in the sun at Golden Gate Park or Dolores Park
  • Hike Land's End for the views
  • Bike through the Presidio, across Golden Gate Bridge, up Hawk Hill
  • Shop in Pac Heights
  • Farmer's markets (Fort Mason, Clement St., Ferry Building)
  • Grab a coffee and explore the neighborhood
  • Venture out of the city if you have time (Mt. Tam, Stinson Beach, Sonoma, Napa, Santa Cruz)
  • Try all the seasonal flavors at Salt & Straw
  • Stay for another week and hit up the Portola music festival
